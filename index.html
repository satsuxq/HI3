Honkai Impact 3rd

Stand against the Honkai. Protect the world.

Honkai Impact 3rd (HI3) is a 3D action RPG developed by miHoYo, the studio that later created Genshin Impact and Honkai: Star Rail. Released in 2016, it is available on PC and mobile devices. You lead a squad of Valkyries, elite fighters who battle the Honkai, a mysterious force that has brought disaster to the world. The game is known for its fast, stylish combat, its anime-inspired art, and a long, emotional story told across many chapters.


Unlike many mobile games, HI3 focuses on real-time action. You do not just pick moves from a menu. You build a team of up to three characters, then dodge, attack, and switch between them freely during battle, which makes every fight feel hands-on. Outside of combat, you can spend time with your Valkyries and equip them with Stigmata in Dorm mode.


The story is set in an alternate version of Earth plagued by catastrophes caused by the Honkai. Part 1 follows Kiana Kaslana and her friends Mei Raiden and Bronya Zaychik as they fight the Honkai under Schicksal, an organization led by Otto Apocalypse. Along the way they meet powerful figures such as Fu Hua, Theresa, Himeko, Kevin, and Durandal, and face Herrschers, beings who carry the Honkai's power. The remnants of the Previous Era, a lost civilization destroyed by the Honkai long ago, also shape events. The story is driven by friendship, sacrifice, and the heavy cost of fighting an inevitable disaster.


The game expanded with Part 2, released on February 29, 2024, which introduces the Dreamseeker, a customizable player avatar who can be male or female.

The Story

Spoiler warning: this part describes the story in general terms, but skip it if you want to experience the game fresh.

The Honkai is a powerful energy that turns people and animals into dangerous monsters, and it returns again and again to threaten humanity. The organization Schicksal fights back with the Valkyries, young women who carry special power in their bodies and wear advanced armor called battlesuits.

The story follows Kiana Kaslana and her friends as they face the Honkai, uncover secrets about the world, and make hard choices about who and what they are willing to protect.

Timeline of Key Events

  1. The Previous Era, an advanced civilization from long ago, is destroyed by the Honkai.
  2. The Honkai resurfaces in the modern age and begins to threaten the world.
  3. Schicksal, led by Otto Apocalypse, forms to fight the Honkai and trains the Valkyries.
  4. Kiana Kaslana meets Mei Raiden, Bronya Zaychik, and other allies, and joins the fight.
  5. Secrets about the Honkai, the Herrschers, and Kiana's own fate come to light.
  6. Hard choices test the loyalty of every character, and sacrifices must be made.

Gameplay and Mechanics

Battles happen in real time. You control one Valkyrie at a time and bring a team of three into each fight. You can swap between them at any moment, so smart players chain the strengths of different characters together into long combos.

Core Mechanics

  • Basic attacks: tap to build a combo. Each Valkyrie has her own style.
  • Special skills: stronger moves with a short cooldown.
  • Evasion: dodge just before an enemy hits to slow time briefly, a moment known as Time Fracture.
  • Ultimates: powerful finishers that spend SP, the skill energy you build up during battle.
  • Squad switching: change Valkyries mid-fight to trigger team skills and extra damage.

Equipment

Each Valkyrie can be equipped with a weapon and a set of three stigmata (top, middle, and bottom pieces). Together they raise her stats and can unlock bonus effects when matched in the right set.

Objectives

Your goals are to clear story chapters, defeat bosses, and climb challenge modes. Along the way you collect materials to level up your Valkyries and gear, making your team stronger for harder fights.

Beginner Steps

  1. Play the main story to unlock your first Valkyries and learn the controls.
  2. Practice dodging. Good timing is the most important skill.
  3. Build a team of three Valkyries that work well together.
  4. Equip weapons and stigmata, then upgrade them.
  5. Try challenge modes once your team is strong enough.

Characters and Modes

Honkai Impact 3rd features a diverse cast of Valkyries, warriors, scientists, and mysterious characters. Each has a unique personality, background, abilities, and connection to the story. Some characters also have multiple battlesuits with different fighting styles.

Featured Characters

Kiana Kaslana

The main protagonist of Honkai Impact 3rd. Kiana is cheerful, energetic, and determined to protect the people she loves. Although she starts as an inexperienced Valkyrie, her journey transforms her into one of the most important heroes in the fight against the Honkai. She is also connected to the Herrscher of the Void and Herrscher of Finality.

Raiden Mei

One of Kiana's closest friends and a skilled swordswoman. Mei is calm, responsible, and caring, especially toward the people important to her. She possesses powerful lightning abilities and becomes the Herrscher of Thunder and later the Herrscher of Origin.

Bronya Zaychik

A highly intelligent and disciplined Valkyrie who specializes in ranged combat and advanced technology. Bronya is known for her calm personality, tactical thinking, and ability to operate powerful weapons and machines. She later becomes the Herrscher of Reason and Herrscher of Truth.

Seele Vollerei

A gentle and soft-spoken girl who deeply cares about her friends, especially Bronya. Seele has a mysterious connection to another personality known as Veliona, who has a more confident and aggressive nature. Their relationship and struggles play an important role in her story.

Fu Hua

An experienced warrior with thousands of years of history behind her. Fu Hua is disciplined, intelligent, and highly skilled in martial arts. She serves as a mentor to younger Valkyries and carries memories connected to the Previous Era. She is also known as the Herrscher of Sentience.

Himeko Murata

A strong-willed instructor at St. Freya Academy and a former captain of the Hyperion. Himeko is known for her confident personality, leadership, and dedication to protecting her students. Despite her tough attitude, she deeply cares about the Valkyries she teaches.

Elysia

A beautiful, cheerful, and playful member of the Thirteen Flame-Chasers from the Previous Era. Elysia is known for her friendly personality, love of beauty, and mysterious past. Despite her playful behavior, she is powerful and deeply values humanity. She is associated with the Herrscher of Human: Ego.

Kevin Kaslana

A legendary warrior from the Previous Era and the leader of the Thirteen Flame-Chasers. Kevin is serious, disciplined, and extremely powerful. He is determined to defeat the Honkai and carries the heavy responsibility of protecting humanity. His actions have a major influence on the history of the Flame-Chasers and the main story.

Welt Yang

A thoughtful and responsible leader associated with Anti-Entropy. Welt is the successor to the title of Herrscher of Reason and possesses the ability to recreate objects through his Herrscher powers. He is intelligent, protective, and willing to make difficult decisions to protect humanity.

Otto Apocalypse

The former Overseer of Schicksal and one of the most influential figures in the game's history. Otto is intelligent, manipulative, and extremely calculating. His actions are largely driven by his desire to bring back Kallen Kaslana, making him a central figure in many of the story's major conflicts.

Theresa Apocalypse

The principal of St. Freya Academy and a powerful Valkyrie connected to the Schicksal organization. Theresa has a cheerful and playful personality, but she takes her responsibilities seriously. She cares deeply for her students and treats them like family.

Senadina

A mysterious and cheerful girl introduced in Part 2 of Honkai Impact 3rd. Senadina is connected to the events unfolding on Mars and becomes an important companion in the Dreamseeker's journey. She fights using a unique yo-yo-inspired weapon and has a mysterious connection to the secrets of the world.

Lylah

A character associated with the Part 2 story. Her background and connections to the events surrounding the Dreamseeker should be explored through her story appearances. She is part of the wider cast introduced during the newer chapters of the game.

Vita

An intelligent and mysterious character first introduced in Part 1.5. Vita is closely connected to Sa and the Sky People, and she often acts according to her own plans. Her confident personality and complicated motives make her an important character in the later story.

Sirin

Known as the Second Herrscher, Sirin is one of the most important figures in the history of the Honkai. Her awakening brought destruction and conflict to humanity. Her tragic past and experiences shaped her hatred toward humanity and played a major role in the events of the story.

Durandal

Also known as Bianka Ataegina, Durandal is one of Schicksal's strongest Valkyries and the captain of the Immortal Blades. She is confident, disciplined, and highly skilled in combat. Despite her strength, she has a friendly and humble personality and always strives to improve herself.

Rita Rossweisse

An elegant and intelligent S-rank Valkyrie who serves Schicksal as a member of the Immortal Blades. Rita is known for her polite manners, calm attitude, and loyalty to her duties. Beneath her graceful appearance is a highly skilled fighter who can be extremely dangerous in battle.

Mobius

One of the Thirteen Flame-Chasers from the Previous Era, Mobius is a brilliant but mysterious scientist associated with the Infinity Signet. She is fascinated by evolution and immortality and often conducts experiments to understand the limits of life. Her snake motif reflects her mysterious and unsettling nature.

Aponia

The third of the Thirteen Flame-Chasers, Aponia is associated with the Discipline Signet. She is a calm and spiritual figure who believes strongly in fate and discipline. Although she appears gentle and caring, her powerful abilities and strict beliefs make her one of the more mysterious Flame-Chasers.

Yae Sakura

A shrine maiden from the Yae Village who is skilled in swordsmanship. Sakura is a quiet and reserved character who deeply cares about her younger sister, Yae Rin. Her story explores themes of sacrifice, love, and the influence of the Honkai on her village.

Guides

Battlesuit Guide

Battlesuits determine a character's combat abilities, weapons, skills, and role in a team. Some focus on dealing damage, while others provide support or control the battlefield. Choosing the right battlesuit depends on your team composition and the enemies you are facing.

Building a Team

A good team combines damage dealers and support battlesuits. Most standard team setups use three characters, with each battlesuit contributing to the team's overall performance.

  • Main damage dealer: The primary battlesuit responsible for dealing most of the team's damage.
  • Support: Helps increase damage through buffs, debuffs, elemental bonuses, or other useful abilities.
  • Secondary support: Provides additional buffs, damage, or utility to complete the team.
  • Match enemy types: Check whether the enemy is Biological, Mechanical, Psychic, Quantum, Imaginary, or Stardust.
  • Check damage type: Choose between Physical, Fire, Ice, and Lightning teams depending on the enemy's weaknesses.

Beginner's Guide

New to Honkai Impact 3rd? These tips can help you understand the game's systems, improve your battlesuits, and progress through the story more comfortably.

  • Follow the main story: Complete story chapters to understand the game's characters, world, and major events. Story stages may also provide progression rewards.
  • Understand battlesuit types: Learn the differences between BIO, MECH, PSY, QUA, IMG, and SD to make better decisions when fighting different enemies.
  • Choose your favorite battlesuits: Explore different characters and combat styles before deciding which ones you want to develop.
  • Upgrade equipment: Weapons and stigmata improve battlesuit performance. Equip items that complement the character's damage type and abilities.
  • Learn enemy attack patterns: Practice dodging, switching characters, and using Ultimate skills at the right moment.
  • Complete daily missions: Daily activities and available challenges provide useful resources and progression rewards.
  • Manage your crystals: Save your crystals for battlesuit or equipment supplies that fit your preferred team.
  • Take advantage of events: Participate in limited-time events to discover additional stories, activities, and rewards.
  • Build complete teams: Remember that support battlesuits are important. A balanced team can perform better than a team consisting only of damage dealers.

Equipment Guide

Equipment plays an important role in improving a battlesuit's performance. The two main equipment categories are weapons and stigmata.

Weapons

Weapons are used by battlesuits to attack enemies and activate special abilities. Different characters use different weapon categories, such as swords, pistols, cannons, bows, and scythes.

Stigmata

Stigmata are equipment items that provide additional attributes and combat bonuses. A complete stigmata set can provide special effects that improve a battlesuit's damage or support abilities.

Equipment Tips

  • Use weapons that match the battlesuit's weapon type.
  • Choose stigmata that complement the character's damage type.
  • Check individual stigmata effects and set bonuses.
  • Upgrade important equipment when you have enough resources.

Combat Guide

Understanding combat mechanics makes battles easier. Learning when to dodge, switch characters, and activate special abilities can greatly improve your performance.

  • Perfect Evasion: Dodge an enemy attack at the correct moment to activate special evasion effects when available.
  • Ultimate Skills: Use powerful attacks when the timing is appropriate to maximize damage.
  • QTE Skills: Some battlesuits can enter the battlefield through special character-switch conditions and perform additional attacks.
  • Elemental Advantages: Build teams that match the enemy's weaknesses and your battlesuit's damage specialization.
  • Team Switching: Switch between characters to activate support effects and maintain your team's combat rotation.

Lore Wiki

Explore the history, organizations, powerful beings, and mysterious events that shape the world of Honkai Impact 3rd. These entries introduce important concepts from the game's storyline and universe.

Spoiler warning: Some entries contain information about major story events, character identities, and revelations from different chapters.

The Honkai

The Honkai is a mysterious force that repeatedly threatens the existence of human civilization. It manifests through disasters, Honkai energy, infected creatures, and powerful enemies. Its influence can corrupt living beings and create dangerous monsters known as Honkai Beasts. Humanity has developed advanced technology and trained Valkyries to resist its destructive influence.

Herrschers

Herrschers are powerful beings connected to the Honkai. Each Herrscher possesses a unique authority or ability that represents a particular aspect of the Honkai. Their powers can affect the physical world in extraordinary ways. Some characters, including Kiana, Mei, and Bronya, eventually obtain Herrscher powers and use them to protect humanity.

The Previous Era

The Previous Era was an advanced civilization that existed thousands of years before the current era. Despite its technological achievements, humanity eventually faced devastating Honkai threats. Its survivors created powerful weapons and organizations in an attempt to preserve humanity. The history of this civilization is closely connected to the Thirteen Flame-Chasers.

The Current Era

The Current Era is the civilization in which the main storyline takes place. Humanity continues to face the Honkai threat while organizations such as Schicksal and Anti-Entropy develop different methods of fighting it. Many of the game's main characters are part of this era, including Kiana Kaslana, Raiden Mei, and Bronya Zaychik.

Schicksal

Schicksal is one of the world's major organizations dedicated to fighting the Honkai. It trains and deploys Valkyries, develops combat technology, and operates facilities around the world. For much of the story, it is controlled by the Apocalypse family, particularly its former Overseer, Otto Apocalypse.

Anti-Entropy

Anti-Entropy is an organization that opposes Schicksal's methods and develops its own technology to combat the Honkai. It is known for its advanced machines, artificial intelligence, and powerful weapons. Important figures associated with the organization include Welt Yang and members of the Cocolia faction.

World Serpent

World Serpent is a secretive organization connected to the survivors and legacy of the Previous Era. Led by Kevin Kaslana, it follows a plan known as Project STIGMA. Its members believe that humanity must undergo a major transformation to survive the Honkai, even if their methods come at a great cost.

The Thirteen Flame-Chasers

The Flame-Chasers were thirteen extraordinary warriors from the Previous Era who fought against the Honkai. Each member possessed unique abilities and carried a special Signet. Important members include Elysia, Kevin, Fu Hua, Mobius, Aponia, and others. Their memories and experiences are explored in the Elysian Realm storyline.

Elysian Realm

The Elysian Realm is a virtual space created to preserve the memories of the Flame-Chasers. It allows visitors to interact with recorded personalities and learn about the Previous Era. The realm plays an important role in uncovering the history of Elysia and the other Flame-Chasers.

Valkyries

Valkyries are specially trained warriors who fight against the Honkai. Many of them possess special compatibility with Honkai energy, allowing them to use powerful weapons and battlesuits. They are primarily associated with Schicksal, although other groups and organizations also have their own fighters.

Honkai Beasts

Honkai Beasts are dangerous creatures created or influenced by Honkai energy. They come in different forms and possess varying levels of strength. Some are relatively weak, while others are powerful enough to threaten entire cities. Valkyries are trained to fight these creatures and protect civilians.

Stigmata

Stigmata are special marks connected to the power of the Honkai and humanity's ability to resist it. In the game, stigmata also appear as equipment that improves a battlesuit's attributes and abilities. Their importance extends beyond equipment, as they are closely connected to the history and future of humanity.

Project STIGMA

Project STIGMA is a major plan developed by Kevin and World Serpent. Its goal is to change humanity to prepare it for the Honkai threat. The project involves the use of artificial Stigmata and has consequences that become central to the later chapters of the main story.

Herrscher of Finality

The Herrscher of Finality represents the final stage of the Herrscher cycle in the Current Era. This power is associated with the culmination of humanity's struggle against the Honkai. Kiana Kaslana becomes the Herrscher of Finality and plays a central role in the conclusion of the main story's Earth arc.

Sea of Quanta

The Sea of Quanta is an extraordinary space beyond ordinary physical reality. It contains countless possible worlds and fragments of different civilizations. Characters who explore it encounter unusual environments and alternate possibilities. It is an important concept in understanding the wider Honkai universe.

Imaginary Tree

The Imaginary Tree is a cosmic structure associated with the existence of different worlds and timelines. Its branches represent different worlds, while its relationship with the Sea of Quanta helps explain the structure of the Honkai universe. It is one of the most important concepts behind the game's larger cosmology.

Part 2: Mars and the New Adventure

Part 2 introduces a new chapter in the Honkai Impact 3rd universe, focusing on the Dreamseeker and a group of characters connected to Mars. The story explores unfamiliar locations, mysterious civilizations, and new secrets involving the nature of reality. Important characters include Senadina, Helia, Coralie, and Vita.

Astral Ring

Astral Ring is a combat system introduced in Part 2. It allows certain battlesuits to activate powerful effects and special abilities during combat. It also introduces additional team-building mechanics that influence how Part 2 battlesuits work together.
